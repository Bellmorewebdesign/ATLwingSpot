# Visual Direction

## Composition rules
- Do not keep every important headline on the left.
- At least one major homepage moment should be fully centered.
- At least one should be right-aligned.
- At least one should use full-bleed photography.
- At least one should use a horizontal product track.
- At least one should overlap type and photography in a controlled way.
- Keep clear reading order on mobile.

## Color
Use ATL cyan and orange as large fields, not only button accents.
Suggested rhythm:
1. Hero: cream/cyan/orange with dark type, or dark with a large cyan/orange brand field.
2. Menu: warm cream.
3. Flavor interaction: full cyan/orange reactive color.
4. Social/story: dark.
5. Locations: light.
6. Final CTA: orange or cyan takeover.

## Food
Food needs to feel larger and closer.
Use:
- macro crops
- full-bleed images
- cutouts breaking type
- texture
- sauce shine
- packaging
- real restaurant/social photography when available

## Motion
Memorable moments:
1. Hero food/type composition that reacts subtly to pointer/scroll.
2. Flavor selector where the entire stage changes color/image/type.
3. Horizontal “crave track” with large products, not cards.
4. Location finder with animated map/list transition.

Do not animate every paragraph.
