# ATL Wing Spot Copy & Voice Guide

## Voice
ATL should sound like a confident young food brand with personality, not a creative agency trying to sound cool.

## Use
- Short statements
- Food-first language
- Specific product names
- Clear ordering language
- Light humor when natural
- Direct calls to action

## Avoid
- “Viral for a reason”
- “Now we’re just getting started”
- “Built for the way people order today”
- “Enough scrolling”
- “You know you want it”
- “Bold flavors. Big energy.”
- “Where flavor meets…”
- “Elevate your…”
- Agency vocabulary like “culture,” “experience,” “journey,” and “ecosystem” unless truly needed
- Numbered editorial labels like “01 — The Lineup”
- Excessive em dashes
- Fake slang

## Better examples
- “Pick your flavor.”
- “Wings. Tenders. Waffles. Let’s eat.”
- “Find your ATL.”
- “Ordering for a crew?”
- “Start with the wings.”
- “25+ flavors. Good luck choosing one.”
- “Fresh wings. Your sauce. Your call.”
- “Catering that actually gets eaten.”
- “Find a location and order.”
- “See the menu.”
