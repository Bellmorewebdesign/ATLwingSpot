# ATL Wing Spot September Client Photo Pack

This folder contains the 20 client-supplied images for the September website refinement.
The filenames have been standardized so they can be referenced safely in code.

## Priority uses

- `01-single-wing-basket.png`: strongest candidate for a cleaner hero food image after a careful background cutout.
- `03-multi-basket-wing-group.png`: group shot to feature near the hero/header area without making the hero overcrowded.
- `04-loaded-waffle-fries.jpg`: use in the "What are we eating?" carousel for a loaded-fries card.
- `05-fruity-pebbles-chicken-waffles.jpg` and `06-oreo-chicken-waffles.jpg`: stronger waffle photography.
- `07-buffalo-ranch-wrap.jpg`, `08-honey-bbq-club-wrap.jpg`, and `09-honey-mustard-wrap.jpg`: wrap/menu photography.
- `10-mac-and-cheese-cup-orange.png` and `11-mac-and-cheese-cup-white.png`: mac-and-cheese imagery.
- `12` through `20`: onion-ring and waffle-fries options for menu cards and supporting sections.

## Quality notes

- `17-onion-rings-orange-box.jpg` and `18-onion-rings-orange-box-duplicate.jpg` are byte-for-byte duplicates. Keep one visible instance and do not make the site repetitive just to display both files.
- Inspect `02-mixed-wing-basket-transparent.png` carefully before use. It has transparency, but parts of the small package lettering may not be strong enough for a prominent placement.
- Optimize the selected assets for the web and preserve responsive image quality. Do not ship all original full-resolution files as page downloads.
- The source pack may remain outside the served build after usable assets are copied into `app/public/assets/food/`.
